# First AC Servicing Client — Discovery Before Build

This file is a **question list**, not validated client requirements. Do not assume the prospective client is an AC servicing company until confirmed.

## Ten questions for the client

1. Do you install AC units, repair them, perform scheduled maintenance, or all three?
2. Office staff and technicians count; should technicians see customer contact details?
3. How do you currently store customers, addresses, equipment and service history? Which exact app/plan or spreadsheet?
4. Can the current system export CSV/Excel? Who is authorised to provide an anonymised sample?
5. What is the one weekly workflow that must work on day one?
6. Do jobs contain one AC unit or multiple? Can multiple technicians be assigned?
7. What information must a completed service report include, and is the customer's signature required?
8. Must it work without internet? Must it send WhatsApp/SMS, or are manual follow-ups acceptable for pilot?
9. Do they need quotations, accounting integration or invoice issuance at launch? If yes, identify provider and API.
10. Who can approve design, supply sample data, test staging, and sign off within each of the next four weeks?

## Details to confirm before user-facing changes

Brand/name, working hours, time zone, languages, field naming, status transitions, recurring interval, file limits, record retention, price, number of users, support expectations and launch acceptance. Record decisions in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` and approved change control.

## Red flags for 4-week deadline

Native/offline sync, unknown proprietary integration, payment processing for end customers, complex multi-tech dispatch, more than one business vertical, custom legacy database migration or unresponsive client approver. Propose narrower pilot if any is unavoidable.
