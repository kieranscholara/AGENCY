## Business outcome

Replace the most important day-to-day service workflow with a usable web application that records customers and equipment, schedules work and records completed visits. Restricted production pilot with a small number of staff; not universal ERP replacement.

## Included (draft)

Company owner login, invited technicians, customers, service locations, AC unit registry, job scheduling/assignment, technician mobile browser workflow, simple checklist and job history, protected company data, operational monitoring/support; CSV import **only** if source export and specification are available in week one.

## Excluded unless signed amendment

Custom accounting/CRM two-way sync, SMS/WhatsApp automation, payment collection for the AC company's customers, AI features, advanced asset/inventory management, route planning, offline/native app, complex recurring billing, multi-company workflows across separate legal entities.

## Inputs/dependencies

Client supplies approver, an anonymised sample, actual service report requirements, role permissions, account/domain ownership decision and test staff. Response deadlines and pricing/support agreement TBD.

## Measurable acceptance

Office user creates customer and unit, schedules visit; assigned tech completes report on phone; history is retrievable. Other company/user access is denied. Imported data (if included) is reconciled with client approval. App passes security/recovery tests and client staging UAT.
